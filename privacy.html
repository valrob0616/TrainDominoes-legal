Privacy Policy — Train Dominoes

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This is the privacy policy for the Train Dominoes app (iOS), published by LFH Inc. (LoudFH).

Who we are

Train Dominoes (“the app”) is a Mexican Train dominoes game for iPhone and iPad. You can play solo against an in-app opponent (the Conductor), or play with up to four people on the same local Wi‑Fi using a short room code. The app does not create user accounts and does not use a game server on the internet.

Contact: rob@loudfh.com
Publisher: LFH Inc. (LoudFH)

What this policy covers

This policy describes how the current version of the app treats information on your device and on a local network during multiplayer.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The app does not include analytics SDKs, advertising, crash-reporting SDKs, or in-app purchases. We do not use App Tracking Transparency because we do not track you across other companies’ apps for advertising.

What stays on your device

The app stores ordinary preferences and play state only on your device (for example via iOS storage such as UserDefaults), including:

  • A random player identifier generated on the device, so the app can tell seats apart at a local table
  • The display name you type for your carriage (optional; used so others at a local table can see a name)
  • Whether sound effects are on
  • Which scenery “route” you are on

Uninstalling the app removes this on-device data. We do not sync it to a cloud account operated by us, because there is no account.

Local multiplayer (same Wi‑Fi)

When you host or join a table, the app uses Apple’s local-network features (Bonjour / Multipeer Connectivity) so nearby devices running Train Dominoes can find each other with a room code. iOS may ask you to allow Local Network access. Bluetooth permission may appear as a fallback when Wi‑Fi cannot carry the table.

During a hand, tile and turn information is exchanged between the devices at that table on the local network. The host device keeps the authority for the tiles for that hand. That traffic is for play at your table. It is not sent to our servers; we do not operate a Train Dominoes backend.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for email, password, contacts, photos, precise GPS, health data, payment cards, or government IDs. It does not create a login. It does not upload your hand history to us. It does not run its own analytics or advertising.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience interested in casual dominoes. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can play solo without using Local Network. You can decline Local Network or Bluetooth permission; multiplayer join/host that needs those features will not work until you allow them in Settings. You can clear the display name or change the sound setting in the app. You can uninstall the app to remove on-device data.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting additional information, or uses information in a new way (for example an online account or analytics), we will update this policy before that behavior ships.
